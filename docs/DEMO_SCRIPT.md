# ShowOnce demo script

## Opening

“I’m doing my master's abroad, while my mom is back home.

Whenever she gets stuck on a complicated website, we end up exchanging screenshots or screen sharing.

I wanted something different.

Instead of controlling her browser, what if I could show her agent once?”

## Product demo — under three minutes

1. Open the ShowOnce dashboard. Point out that this is a product for reusable task handoffs, not a story site.
2. Click **New ShowOnce**, choose **Northstar Benefits Demo**, and start showing.
3. In Samuel’s account, keep Gold Dental, choose annual renewal, confirm the address step, enable paperless, preview, and finish.
4. Show the generated semantic procedure: carry over, adapt, always ask, and never included.
5. Create a seven-day handoff for Mom and open the recipient view.
6. In a WebMCP-capable browser, ask the external agent: “Do what Samuel showed me.”
7. Show real tool calls in the WebMCP inspector while Mom’s account updates.
8. Highlight adaptation: Gold Dental exists, address update is skipped, dependents stay untouched, annual and paperless are safe, and $142 versus $88 is a material difference.
9. Check the personal attestation box in Mom’s UI and click **Confirm & submit** once — this is the only human step, and it both attests and submits atomically; no further agent turn is needed.
10. Show the completion summary and audit activity.
11. Reset to **Plan unavailable**. Run comparison again, click **Ask Samuel**, recommend Silver, and continue. Emphasize that the system stopped instead of guessing.

## Closing

“One person shows. Another person’s agent adapts. No account sharing, no remote control, and no pretending meaningful differences do not exist.”
